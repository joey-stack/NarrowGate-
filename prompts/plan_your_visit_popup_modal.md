# Implementation Prompt: Plan Your Visit Pop-Up Modal

## Objective
Build an interactive, bilingual **Plan Your Visit** pop-up modal (`PlanVisitModal.tsx`) inspired by Passion Creek & Elevation Church visitor booking experiences.

## Execution Steps
1. Create `PlanVisitContext.tsx` and `PlanVisitModal.tsx`.
2. Update `en.json` and `it.json` with modal translations.
3. Wrap app in `PlanVisitProvider`.
4. Connect Hero, Header, and Gathering Cards CTAs to launch modal with gathering presets.
5. Run `npm run build` to verify.
6. Commit and deploy to Vercel production.
